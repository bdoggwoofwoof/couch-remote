First time? Allow the TV's security certificate

LG TVs use a self-signed certificate, so your browser has to be told to trust it once per TV.

  1. Tap Open the TV's address.
  2. You'll see a privacy warning. On iPhone tap Show Details → visit this website. On Android tap Advanced → Proceed.
  3. A blank or error page after that is normal. Come back here and tap Connect.
  4. Accept the request that pops up on the TV with your physical remote. You only do this once.

If your browser asks to access devices on your local network, choose Allow.
